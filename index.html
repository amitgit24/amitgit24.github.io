About

Building learning systems that connect perception, reasoning and physical action.

I am an MS Research student in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras. My work combines machine learning with robotics, particularly foundation models for robotic manipulation.

My current interests include Vision-Language-Action models, multimodal learning, imitation learning, diffusion and flow-matching policies, human-to-robot transfer, robotic simulation, and deployment of learned policies on physical robots.

Research

Research interests

01

Vision-Language-Action Models

02

Human-to-Robot Transfer

Robot Learning

Imitation learning, diffusion policies, flow matching, action chunking and representation learning for manipulation tasks.

04

Simulation & Sim-to-Real

Simulation-based policy development, digital twins, policy evaluation and transfer between simulated and physical robotic systems.
